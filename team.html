Our story

At OurWelcomeHome, we believe that comfort, clarity, and care make all the difference in a stay. We're a multilingual couple based in Sheikh Zayed, offering a curated selection of fully furnished apartments for travelers, professionals, and families looking for a space that truly feels like home.

As frequent travelers ourselves, we started hosting over eight years ago with a simple goal: to create the kind of experience we always looked for — welcoming, reliable, and thoughtfully designed. Since then, we've proudly maintained a near-perfect rating on Airbnb, built on consistency, responsiveness, and personal attention.

Every detail of our listings is carefully chosen — from location to layout — with an understanding of what makes a stay feel effortless, whether you're visiting with children, arriving late after a long flight, or needing a peaceful place to work and recharge.

We're available in Arabic, English, and German, and you'll have our direct contact throughout your stay — with no hidden fees or surprises.
