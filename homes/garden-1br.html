Karma Residence Compound · Sheikh Zayed

Top 1% Airbnb 1BR + Private Garden in Compound

1 bedroom·Sleeps 3·Private garden·Step-free entry

A ground-floor one-bedroom inside Karma Residence, with its own garden and its own entrance. Both living spaces look onto the greenery, and the garden gate is the way in — self check-in through a lockbox, no stairs anywhere on the route from the street.

The bedroom has a 160 cm-wide bed and the living-room sofa converts into another 160 cm bed, so the home sleeps three. An air mattress is available as well; three guests remains the maximum. Two Smart TVs, and an extendable dining table that doubles as a desk.

  • Private garden with seating and a sun umbrella, and your own gate
  • Kitchen with gas hob, oven, microwave, fridge-freezer, kettle, filter coffee machine and blender
  • Washing machine and a separate tumble dryer
  • Air conditioning that heats as well as cools, in both rooms
  • Step-free from the street parking, through the gate, into the home

Wi-Fi runs at about 28 Mbps, with 35 GB a week or 140 GB a month included. Standard cable is free; streaming is on your own accounts. A crib or stroller can be added for USD 5 a night if you ask in advance.

The garden is yours alone for the stay. The compound streets and lawns are shared residential grounds. One camera sits above the garden gate and sees only the approach to it: nothing of the garden, nothing indoors.
