The neighbourhood

Village West by Dorra offers a fresh residential setting in Sheikh Zayed, with landscaped grounds and a shared playground to enjoy during your stay. Spend time exploring the neighbourhood’s cafés, restaurants and shopping by car, then return to the quieter setting of the compound and your own private garden and terrace. It is a welcoming base for enjoying Sheikh Zayed and exploring western Cairo.

Getting around

One assigned underground parking space and free street parking inside the compound are available. A car or ride-hailing service is useful for exploring Sheikh Zayed and visiting sights across western Cairo. Allow extra time for traffic when planning outings and airport transfers.
