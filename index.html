Why guests come

Close to the reasons people actually come to Zayed.

Family, first of all

Most of our guests come to see family in Zayed and 6th of October — and need a whole home nearby rather than a hotel room. Visitors are welcome until 10 PM, and there's room for everyone at the table.

Work that can't wait

Smart Village and Capital Business Park are fifteen minutes away, every home has fibre internet and a proper desk — and Sheikh Zayed isn't on Egypt's load-shedding schedule, so the call doesn't drop when the power would.

Treatment and recovery

Dar Al Fouad, Baheya, Rofayda and El Nada are all minutes away. A quiet home with a full kitchen, weekly cleaning and everything delivered to the door is a gentler place to recover than a hotel room — for the patient, and for the family staying with them.

Shopping and days out

Mall of Arabia in ten minutes, Arkan Plaza and Americana for dinner, Galleria and the fruit market round the corner — and everything else delivered by Talabat, Rabbit or Breadfast within the hour.

Good to know

The four things everyone asks.

Everything else lives in the house manual, and in the WhatsApp group we open the day your booking is confirmed — a late flight, an early arrival, a gate that needs warning. The basket on the table takes care of itself.

How do I actually get in?

No keys to collect and nobody to chase. Your house manual arrives as soon as we have your documents, and the lock-box code lands 24 hours before you fly.

When can I arrive?

From 3 PM, and out by noon. Earlier or later is often fine — ask us and we'll see what the cleaning schedule allows.

What if something needs fixing?

Message the WhatsApp group we open for your stay — it's the fastest way to reach us. Emad looks after repairs and we'll get someone to you as quickly as we can.

Can we have visitors?

Yes — family and friends are welcome until 10 PM. Visits are for family gatherings: we live in a small, close-knit community, so we ask guests to respect Egyptian and Islamic custom, and we do not host mixed-gender gatherings of unmarried guests.
