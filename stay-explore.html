Discover Sheikh Zayed City

Located just outside of Cairo, Sheikh Zayed City is a modern, elegant suburb that blends tranquility with convenience. It's the ideal destination for travelers looking for a peaceful stay with easy access to the city's top attractions, shopping, and business hubs.

  • Top shopping & dining: vibrant malls like Mall of Arabia (10 min), Mall of Egypt (20 min), Arkan Plaza, and Americana Plaza — international brands, cinemas, fine dining, and local gems.
  • Business & innovation hubs: minutes from Smart Village and Capital Business Park — a favorite for business travelers and remote workers.
  • Green spaces: tree-lined streets, gated communities, and landscaped parks, perfect for morning walks or unwinding after a long day.
  • Egypt's iconic sites: only 25 minutes to the Great Pyramids of Giza and the Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) — the world's largest archaeological museum. Downtown Cairo is 35–40 minutes away.
  • Family-friendly & safe: secure compounds, top schools, clinics, and a relaxed pace — ideal for families, solo travelers, and digital nomads alike.
  • Reliable power: our neighborhood is not affected by Egypt's scheduled power outages — uninterrupted comfort during your stay.

Getting around

Ride-hailing

Uber, Careem, and inDrive all work seamlessly in our area — download from the App Store or Google Play and you're ready to go.

Airport transfers

  • Cairo International Airport is about 50–60 minutes away, depending on traffic.
  • Sphinx International Airport is closer — just 25–30 minutes.
  • For pickup or drop-off, book the Cairo Airport Shuttle Service (reliable, fixed-rate, optional meet & greet) — or ask us to arrange the trusted private driver we use ourselves. Just send us your arrival details.

Food & shopping delivery

Getting meals, groceries, or essentials delivered is fast and easy in Sheikh Zayed:

  • Food: Talabat (food & groceries), Elmenus (wide variety of local restaurants)
  • Groceries: Instashop (also delivers from pharmacies), Rabbit (fast essentials), Breadfast (quality grocery & bakery)
  • General shopping: Amazon Egypt, Noon, Jumia

Frequently asked questions

Bookings & payments

How do I book a stay?
Book directly through our website for the best rates and no platform fees — secure credit card payment at the time of booking. We're also available on Airbnb.

Do you require a deposit?
For direct bookings, a refundable deposit equal to 2 nights (minimum $100) is required; it's refunded after your stay, provided the apartment is left in good condition. For Airbnb bookings no deposit is required, but Airbnb charges service fees.

Can I modify or cancel my reservation?
We follow Airbnb's Strict Cancellation Policy — see the cancellation policy. The same applies to modifications, which depend on availability at the time.

Will I receive a booking confirmation?
Yes — confirmations are sent once full payment is completed and the required ID documents are provided (see ID requirements in the Terms & Conditions). Your confirmation includes check-in instructions and all relevant reservation information.

Do you offer long-term or repeat stay discounts?
Yes! We love hosting repeat guests — message us for exclusive recurring-guest offers. Long-term discounts are already embedded in the price of your stay.

During your stay

What time is check-in and check-out?
Check-in from 3:00 PM, check-out by 12:00 PM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available on request, depending on availability.

Do you offer cleaning during the stay?
For longer stays we offer mid-stay cleaning — see optional extras. Contact us in advance to schedule.

Are visitors allowed?
Visits are allowed for family gatherings only; we do not permit gatherings of unmarried individuals of mixed gender. We expect guests to respect Egyptian, Eastern, and Islamic customs and traditions — we live in a small, close-knit community and prefer not to raise concerns among neighbors.

Is Wi-Fi available?
Yes — all our properties offer high-speed Wi-Fi free of charge. See internet upgrades for faster options.

Location & access

Where are your properties located?
In safe, peaceful gated compounds in Sheikh Zayed City, Egypt, with easy access to shopping, dining, and major roads — see the area guide.

Is parking available?
Yes — secure parking is free of charge at all of our properties.

How do I access the property?
You'll receive a detailed house manual including check-in instructions once we receive the required documentation (needed for compound security). The lock-box code is sent 24 hours before arrival. Please take your time with the house manual — it holds valuable information.
