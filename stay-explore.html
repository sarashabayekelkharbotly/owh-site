Everything is close

Sheikh Zayed sits just outside Cairo — far enough for quiet streets and gardens, close enough that nothing worth seeing is a trek. Typical drive times from our homes:

10minutesMall of Arabia
15minutesSmart Village & Capital Business Park
20minutesGrand Egyptian Museum
25minutesThe Great Pyramids of Giza
25–30minutesSphinx International Airport
35–40minutesDowntown Cairo

Cairo International Airport is roughly 50–60 minutes away, depending on traffic.

Why guests like it here

Shopping & dining

Mall of Arabia, Mall of Egypt, Arkan Plaza and Americana Plaza — international brands, cinemas, fine dining and local gems, all within a short drive.

A base for business

Minutes from Smart Village and Capital Business Park, with fibre internet and a work desk in every home — a favourite with business travellers and remote workers.

Green and quiet

Tree-lined streets, gated communities and landscaped parks. Our compounds are made for morning walks and evenings outside, not traffic noise.

Safe and family-friendly

Secure compounds with 24/7 security, excellent hospitals and clinics nearby, good schools, and plenty for children to do at a relaxed pace.

The power stays on

Our neighbourhood is not affected by Egypt's scheduled power cuts, so your stay isn't interrupted — worth knowing if you are working through the day.

Hosts who live here

We're a short drive away, we speak Arabic, English and German, and you'll have our direct contact from the moment you book.

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a stay?

Book directly through this website for the best rates and no platform fees, paying securely by card at the time of booking. We're on Airbnb too, but booking direct is always cheaper for you.

Do you require a deposit?

For direct bookings, a refundable deposit of USD 100, returned after your stay provided the home is left in good condition. Airbnb bookings need no deposit but carry Airbnb's service fees.

Can I change or cancel my reservation?

We follow Airbnb's Strict Cancellation Policy — see the cancellation policy for the exact windows. Changes depend on availability at the time.

Will I get a booking confirmation?

Yes — once full payment is completed and your ID documents are provided. It includes check-in instructions and all your reservation details.

Do you offer long-stay or repeat-guest discounts?

Long-stay discounts are already built into the price you see. We love hosting repeat guests — message us for our recurring-guest offers.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 3:00 PM, check-out by 12:00 PM. Early or late times are sometimes possible — just ask.

Are visitors allowed?

Visits are for family gatherings, and visitors are welcome until 10 PM. We don't permit gatherings of unmarried individuals of mixed gender. We live in a small, close-knit community and ask guests to respect Egyptian, Eastern and Islamic customs.

How do I get in?

Once we have your documents we send a detailed house manual with check-in instructions, and the lock-box code arrives 24 hours before you land. The manual is worth reading — it holds a lot of useful detail.

What if I need help during my stay?

You'll be added to a dedicated WhatsApp group as soon as your booking is confirmed. Message any time — we answer quickly.

Where can I follow you?

On Instagram, Facebook and Google.
