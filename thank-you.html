What happens next

  1. A confirmation email and invoice arrive once your payment has gone through. If it hasn't reached you within a few minutes, check your spam folder before worrying.
  2. Send us your ID or passport for each guest. Egyptian law and the compound's own rules require this before we can register you with security — your booking isn't final until we have it.
  3. We'll add you to a WhatsApp group for your stay. That's the fastest way to reach us with anything at all, before or during your visit.
  4. Your house manual follows, with directions, check-in instructions and the details that make arriving easy. It's worth a read.
  5. The lock-box code arrives 24 hours before you land, so you can let yourself in whenever you get here.
