House rules

The short version of everything below: treat the home as you would your own, and respect the neighbours we live among.

  1. Only registered guests may stay. Visitors are welcome until 10 PM. Extra guests must be approved with ID or passport. For Arab guests, mixed-gender couples must be married.
  2. No parties, events, or illegal substances.
  3. No smoking indoors — backyard or balcony only.
  4. No pets allowed.
  5. Respect quiet hours, 5 PM to 10 AM.
  6. No eating or drinking in the bedrooms.
  7. Turn off the air conditioning when you leave.
  8. Respect check-in (3 PM) and check-out (12 PM). Extensions may be possible with notice.
  9. Treat the furniture with care — please don't rearrange it; damages may be charged.
  10. Lost keys or access cards: $50.
  11. Keep the home clean — wash dishes and take trash to the sidewalk bins.
  12. Do not flush paper or waste down the toilet.
  13. Toddler amenities are available — just contact us in advance.

Cancellation policy

We follow Airbnb's Strict Cancellation Policy. In plain terms:

Full refund

Cancel within 48 hours of booking, as long as check-in is still at least 14 days away.

50% refund

After that window, up to 7 days before check-in — excluding the service fee.

No refund

Less than 7 days before check-in, and for no-shows or early departures.

The same applies to changes to a reservation, which depend on availability at the time. We recommend confirming your travel plans before booking; travel insurance is a good idea for added peace of mind.
